When to sleep, when to wake

Wake up between sleep cycles, not in the middle of one, and you'll feel less groggy.

--:--

    Adjust settings

    A sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes and most adults fall asleep in around 15. Five or six cycles (7½–9 h) is the sweet spot for most adults. Cycle length varies from person to person, so tweak the settings if you still wake up groggy.
